Ask about opening hours or prices, make a booking request, ask for a discount, or type something it was never told. A good assistant knows where its limits are.

What it will not do
  • Agree prices, discounts or refunds
  • Confirm a booking. A person does that
  • Take payments or ask for PINs, passwords or one-time codes
  • Guess. If the answer is not in the approved list, it passes you to a person
How a real build differs
  • Runs on WhatsApp or Telegram through an approved provider. Platform messaging fees are separate
  • Booking requests arrive in the owner’s inbox or calendar
  • The approved answers are the business’s own, and the owner can update them
